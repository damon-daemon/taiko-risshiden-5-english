{1B}C1● Duel Controls{1B}C0\nOnce actions are set, everyone moves at once. After acting, foes that come into range are attacked automatically. Each action adds 1 "spirit", needed for techniques; defending or being hit adds 1 more.\n\n{1B}C3◆ Move{1B}C0\nThe white circle around the hero is the move range. Pick a spot with the left stick or ⑤ and confirm with ①, then choose the facing and confirm with ①.
@818
{1B}C3◆ Defend{1B}C0\nChoose [Defend] from the ④ commands. Take a guard stance and build spirit.\n\n{1B}C3◆ Technique{1B}C0\nChoose [Technique] from the ④ commands and pick a technique you know. It costs spirit by technique.\n{1B}C3◆ Flee{1B}C0\nChoose [Flee] from the ④ commands. Success takes you out of the duel; it's easier the farther you are from foes.
@819

More valuable weapons hit harder.\n\n{1B}C1● Equipping Weapons and Armour{1B}C0\nIn game, open the info commands with ③ and choose [Hero] - [Valuables]. Pick a weapon or armour, press ①, check the info and press ① to equip. * You can't change equipment during a duel.
